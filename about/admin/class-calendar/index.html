Read about the Game Project

Form your Lab group on eDimension by Monday, 21 September 2026, 23:59.

  • Maximum 2 students per group
  • Pair work is optional
  • If you are completing the labs individually, still enrol in a group containing only yourself. Otherwise, you will not be able to submit the assignment on eDimension.

Lessons​

SessionTopics / Materials
Lecture 1Introduction: course administration, background, and past-year samples. Brief History of Video Games: video game consoles and popular video games from 1972 to today. macOS game sample · Windows game sample
Lecture 2Core Game Mechanics: reflex, timing, precision and accuracy, management, strategy, measurement, tactical choice, puzzle, exploration, and social interaction. Formal Elements: the fundamental building blocks and structural components that define how a game is set up and played.
LabGame Design Process & Best Practices: GDD, ideation, prototyping, iteration, and playtesting, Unity for Newborns

Lessons​

SessionTopics / Materials
Lecture 1Game Genres: how combinations of core mechanics give rise to game genres and subgenres.
Lecture 2General Principles of Game Design: player empathy, feedback, grounding the player, moment-to-moment experience, removing impediments, structure, and progression.
Lecture 3Octalysis: a gamification framework based on eight core drives: Epic Meaning & Calling, Development & Accomplishment, Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback, Ownership & Possession, Social Influence & Relatedness, Scarcity & Impatience, Unpredictability & Curiosity, and Loss & Avoidance. This is done during lab time.
LabWeek 1 Lab Presentation: 15-30 minutes. Unity for Babies · Unity for Toddlers · Lab 1 Review
