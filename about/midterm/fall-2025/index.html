Fall 2025

As usual, we conducted a 48-hour game jam as part of our midterm examination. It was a painful process for some, and an exciting process for some others.

🎉🍾 Here we commemorate the efforts our students have poured into 2025-FALL Midterm Examination. We had lots of fun grading the exam and we hope you have sharpened your game design and development skills through it. Thank you for your hard work and contribution.

The list are sorted alphabetically from the titles of selected games made in 2025 Midterm "Game Jam".
